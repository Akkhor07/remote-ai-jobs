Stay safe

Watch out for fake recruiters

Scammers often copy real AI job platforms. These quick checks will keep you safe.

  • Real platforms never charge you to apply, train, or “unlock” a job.
  • Check that emails come from the company’s official domain.
  • Be careful with WhatsApp or Telegram “recruiters” promising guaranteed work.
  • We will never ask you for money, passwords, or ID documents.
Questions

FAQ

Can I apply from my country?

Many roles accept applicants from lots of countries, but the rules differ by company and role. Check the job page to confirm your country is eligible before you apply.

Do I need to be an AI expert?

No. Many AI companies need people with real-world knowledge in fields like law, finance, medicine, writing, languages or coding to help train and test their models.

Does it cost anything?

Never. Applying is free. If anyone asks you to pay for a job, it’s a scam.

Who hires me?

You apply to and work with the company listing the job. We just find the good ones and share them with you.

How do I hear about new jobs?

Follow the LinkedIn page. New roles are posted there and added here every week.
